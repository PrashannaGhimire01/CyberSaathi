## A. Participant Information Sheet

**Study title:** Evaluating CyberSaathi, an AI-assisted scam-detection app for
non-technical smartphone users in Nepal.

**Researcher:** Prashan Ghimire.

**What is this study about?**
You are invited to help evaluate a mobile app that checks whether a message or
link might be a scam and explains why. The goal is to see whether the app and its
explanations help people recognise scams.

**What will I be asked to do?**
In one session of about 25–30 minutes you will: (1) judge some example messages
as "scam" or "safe"; (2) use the app to check some messages and read its
explanations; (3) try a few short lessons; (4) judge more example messages; and
(5) answer a short feedback questionnaire.

**Do I have to take part?**
No. Participation is completely voluntary. You may skip any question and may stop
at any time, without giving a reason and without any consequence.

**Are there any risks?**
No real personal messages, accounts or contacts are used — all examples are
created by the researcher. The topic is scams, so at the end you will receive a
short "how to stay safe" summary.

**Will my information be kept private?**
Yes. You will be identified only as "Participant 1–8." No names are recorded. Data
is stored on an encrypted, password-protected device and used only for this
dissertation. It will be deleted after [retention period].

**Contact:** [gimire.prashanna2@gmail.com]. If you have concerns about how the study was conducted,
contact [ethics committee / supervisor].

## G. Facilitator script

1. **Welcome:** "Thank you for helping. This will take about 25 minutes. There
   are no right or wrong answers about *you* — we're testing the app, not you.
   You can stop any time." → give Information Sheet, obtain consent.
2. **Pre-test:** "Here are 8 messages. For each, tell me if you think it's a scam
   or safe, and how sure you are." Record; give no feedback.
3. **App use (RQ1):** "Now we'll check some messages in the app." For WITH items:
   "Read what the app says, then tell me in your own words why it's risky." For
   WITHOUT items: "Look only at the risk level — don't scroll down yet — and tell
   me why you think it's risky." Score 0/1/2.
4. **Lessons:** "Try these short lessons at your own pace."
5. **Post-test:** "Here are 8 more messages — same as before, scam or safe, and
   how sure." Record.
6. **Questionnaire:** hand over / read aloud, record answers.
7. **Debrief:** thank them, give the safety summary, answer any questions.
